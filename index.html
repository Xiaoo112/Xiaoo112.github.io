About

I'm a PhD student at Clemson University (GPA 3.9) working on deep learning methods for medical image analysis. My work spans benchmarking MIL algorithms across imaging modalities, building production-ready inference pipelines, and extending self-supervised learning with geometric consistency objectives.

I hold an M.S. in Biostatistics from NYU and a dual B.S. in Mathematics & Statistics from the University of Iowa. I care about rigorous evaluation — not just model accuracy, but calibration, generalizability, and clinical relevance.
